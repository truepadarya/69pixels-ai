# 69pixels

> 69pixels designs brand identities, websites and digital products. AI-native senior leads bring strategy, creative direction, design and development together, from first idea to launch.

Hello, agent. Here is the short version of our site, without the animation. This guide describes the work, services and ways to get in touch shown on the homepage.

Our approach combines senior design judgement with AI-assisted research, prototyping, visual production, coding and custom tools. Human taste and creative direction guide the work. We help with new launches, redesigns and ongoing product and brand work.

The examples below describe individual projects. Their timelines are case-specific, rather than delivery promises for a new brief. Scope, timing and commercial terms are agreed with the team.

## Ways to work together

- [Fixed project](/#work-setup-title): A defined scope, timeline and fixed fee. Deliverables are agreed before kickoff; the team takes ownership through handoff.
- [Dedicated subscription](/#work-setup-title): An embedded team with design, art direction and project management for ongoing priorities. The homepage currently lists EUR 6,900 per month; confirm the current scope and rate with the team.
- [Custom partnership](/#work-setup-title): A team and commercial setup agreed around the business, including specialist expertise, multiple workstreams or a blend of projects and ongoing support.

## Selected work

- [Healthcare app prototypes for INI Hannover](/#cases): Four clickable concepts built with Figma Make for testing. 69pixels worked on the architecture, user flows and screen logic, then refined the generated output. The case includes NurseCopilot for staff tablets and shows a seven-day project timeline.
- [SwapSquad product discovery](/#cases): Personas and user scenarios brought together in a shared discovery workspace, covering steps, decisions and friction in the product's journeys.
- [Marketplace visual production tool](/#cases): A custom prompt-generation tool that turns an uploaded product card, target style and preservation requirements into a repeatable brief for AI-assisted visuals. The case includes the tool interface and before-and-after examples for a candle, backpack, headphones and projector.
- [Cross-platform work planner](/#cases): AI-assisted UI kit work and reusable components helped adapt an existing planning app for Android. Designers refined platform-specific layouts and interactions.
